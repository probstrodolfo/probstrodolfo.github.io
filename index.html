CURIOSITY, FROM THE GROUND UP

Big questions.
A world of small discoveries.

Why do similar adaptations evolve again and again? How do species diversify? What can the smallest organisms tell us about the living world?

We combine field biology, systematics, and genomics to investigate the origins and evolution of biodiversity. Ants and their partnerships with plants are central to our work—and our curiosity extends across the tree of life.
