approach:
  label: Teaching approach
  title: Students learn science by doing it.
  paragraphs:
    - >-
      Teaching in the Probst Lab combines close observation with hands-on investigation. Students
      work with organisms, specimens, data, and molecular tools to ask biological questions,
      evaluate evidence, and explain what their results mean.
  showCycle: false
  cycle:
    - number: '01'
      title: Observe
      description: Begin with organisms, patterns, specimens, and phenomena that invite careful attention.
    - number: '02'
      title: Question
      description: Turn curiosity into biological questions that can be examined with evidence.
    - number: '03'
      title: Investigate
      description: Use field, laboratory, computational, and comparative methods with purpose.
    - number: '04'
      title: Communicate
      description: Explain findings clearly, evaluate uncertainty, and connect results to larger ideas.
