We are building a research community at UC Davis around evolutionary biology, biodiversity, and natural history. Our work combines field observations with morphology, phylogenomics, and sequencing technologies.

## Start a conversation

If our questions resonate with you, email [Rodolfo Probst](mailto:probstrodolfo@gmail.com) with a brief introduction, the questions that interest you, and the kind of research experience you are looking for.

**Undergraduate students:** Tell us what you would like to explore or learn, your relevant coursework or experience, and your availability. Curiosity and a willingness to learn matter.

**Prospective graduate students and postdoctoral researchers:** Share your research interests, CV, and anticipated timeline so we can discuss scientific fit and potential opportunities.

**Collaborators:** We welcome conversations that connect organisms, approaches, and ideas across disciplines.

## A community of learners

We value careful science, collaboration, inclusion, and opportunities for students to develop their own questions. Read more about [mentorship]({{ '/mentorship/' | relative_url }}) and [inclusion]({{ '/DEI/' | relative_url }}).
